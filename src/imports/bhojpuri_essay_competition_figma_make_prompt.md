Core objective:
This is not a generic promo page. It is a formal yet inspiring competition microsite for students and colleges. It should explain the initiative clearly, create trust quickly, and make it easy for institutions to circulate the notice.

Safe Sea Group is a global shipping and logistics group based in Mumbai and operating internationally since 2006.

- Premium Indian cultural aesthetic

- Elegant ornamental borders inspired by Indian folk and manuscript aesthetics
- Modern typography with strong hierarchy
- Clean layout, spacious sections, excellent readability
- The page should feel alive and graceful, with smooth scroll animations, subtle parallax, gentle fade-ins, soft hover states, and refined motion

- Use tasteful decorative motifs. Avoid clutter, neon colors, and gimmicky effects
- Make the site feel premium enough for an institution to trust and forward without hesitation

Mobile and usability:
- Mobile-first design
- Fully responsive
- Large readable text
- Easy tap targets
- Clear visual separation between sections
